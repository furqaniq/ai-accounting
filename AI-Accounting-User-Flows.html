01

Auth & tenancy

Sign in, keep a session, switch organizations, and prove that another tenant's rows are unreachable from both the UI and direct API calls.

login · org switch · tenancy/RBAC
  1. Credentials entered
    Email and password post to /auth/login. Successful login returns a JWT access token, a rotating refresh token, and the caller's memberships.
  2. Tenant chosen per request
    Every tenant request carries Authorization: Bearer … and X-Organization-Id. The server validates membership; it never trusts a client-supplied organization id.
  3. Scoped data access
    TenantContextService puts the request in an AsyncLocalStorage context. ScopedDataAccess applies the organization filter to every tenant-owned model; direct Prisma paths still filter org and row-lock.
  4. Organization switch
    A multi-org user switches from the spine without re-login. The active role is resolved server-side and the UI re-reads the new tenant's records.
  5. Guard-rail outcomes
    Missing or invalid token: 401. Foreign organization header: 403. Cross-tenant document id: 404. Viewer writes: 403.
    Wrong password stays on /login with “Invalid email or password.” and never creates a session.
Books & guarantees
Writes
None; auth and tenancy are read/guard paths.
Isolation
Every query is organization-scoped; two seeded orgs test it.
Roles
Owner, Admin, Accountant, Staff, Viewer.

02

Invoice lifecycle

Estimate to accepted quote, converted draft, sent invoice, posted receivable, applied receipt, then AR aging and profit & loss.

estimates · invoices · payments · AR
  1. Estimate drafted and sent
    EST-#### starts DRAFT, moves to SENT. Lines snapshot item code, description, quantity, rate, and tax. Nothing posts yet.
  2. Accepted and converted
    Only ACCEPTED estimates convert. Conversion copies the customer and lines into one DRAFT INV-####, stores the unique convertedInvoiceId, and posts no journal.
    Repeat conversion returns the existing invoice number.
  3. Send posts the ledger
    Sending is idempotent and posts once: debit AR 1100, credit income accounts, credit sales tax 2100. The PDF endpoint returns application/pdf as INV-####.pdf.
    Draft only edits are allowed before send; sent entries are immutable and corrected by reversal.
  4. Receipt applied
    One RECEIVED PAY-#### can apply across several open invoices for one customer. Receipt total must equal the applied sum. Row locks reject over-application, drafts, paid invoices, and mixed customers.
  5. Status and statements
    Partial sets PARTIAL; full sets PAID and paidAt. Payment posts Cash 1000 debit / AR credit once. AR aging uses invoice issueDate and payment date; P&L reads posted ledger balances.
Books & guarantees
Numbers
Per-org EST-####, INV-####, PAY-####.
One writer
LedgerEngine is the only journal writer.
Statuses
DRAFT / SENT / PARTIAL / PAID; OVERDUE is derived.
Retention
Payments create-only; no unapplied credit yet.

03

Expense & bill lifecycle

Quick expense with a receipt in object storage, plus vendor bill from draft to open, payment, and AP aging.

expenses · attachments · bills · AP
  1. Expense recorded
    EXP-#### requires an active EXPENSE category, date, two-decimal amount, optional active vendor, and memo. Creating posts category debit / Cash 1000 credit once.
  2. Receipt attached
    Uploads are validated by extension, MIME, and magic bytes, capped at 10 MB, and sanitized. AttachmentStore owns MinIO; object keys include org and entity. Downloads use 15-minute presigned URLs.
  3. Expense corrected
    Financial edits reverse the prior entry and post a corrected :vN entry. Memo/vendor-only edits stay off the ledger.
  4. Bill drafted and opened
    VEND-#### then BILL-#### DRAFT. Opening row-locks the bill, validates active AP 2000 and purchase tax 1300, then posts expense debits + tax debit + AP credit once and sets OPEN.
  5. Bill paid and aged
    One SENT BPAY-#### applies across OPEN/PARTIAL bills for one vendor. Payment posts AP debit / Cash credit once. AP aging subtracts applications dated on or before the as-of date and excludes drafts.
Books & guarantees
Expense
Category debit / Cash credit; correction pairs are visible.
Bill
Expense + tax debit / AP credit; only DRAFT is editable.
Payables
Vendor balance = non-draft total less amount paid.
Receipts
PDF/PNG/JPEG/WebP only; 10 MB cap; org-scoped.

04

Bank import & reconciliation

Preview and map a statement, import normalized rows, categorize or match them, then start, clear, complete, and undo a reconciliation session.

import · rules · match · reconcile
  1. Preview and map
    POST /bank-imports/preview parses CSV/OFX up to 10 MB, detects headers and delimiters, maps date/description/amount or debit/credit, and normalizes UTC dates plus signed amounts (positive in, negative out). No rows are written.
  2. Import batch persisted
    POST /bank-imports skips duplicates by external id or date+amount+description, stores a BankImportBatch with row/imported/duplicate/invalid counts, and links every transaction to the batch. Each new row emits a domain event after commit.
  3. Category, match, or resolve
    Categorization rules apply at import time (case-insensitive, whitespace-normalized, lower priority then older rule) and post one BANK journal. Matching links an existing open invoice, bill, payment, or expense without posting or changing it. Resolution can categorize, transfer between bank accounts, create an expense, or create a customer payment — one time, row-locked, audited.
  4. Session starts
    One session per organization/bank account/period snapshots the statement ending balance, ledger opening balance before the period, and book balance at period end. The difference is statement minus cleared balance.
  5. Clear, complete, undo
    Clearing is book-direction aware and recalculates immediately; a transaction cleared elsewhere cannot be cleared again. Completion requires zero difference and locks the session. Undo is COMPLETED-only, restores the completion snapshot, and returns the session to ACTIVE.
    Discrepancy publishes a reconciliation event after commit when the difference is non-zero.
Books & guarantees
Signs
Positive is money in; negative is money out.
Rules
Exactly one rule per transaction; edits never rewrite imports.
Matching
Link-only; no journal and no record mutation.
Reconcile
Active-only toggles; complete at zero; undo restores snapshot.
Evidence
  • tests/e2e/banking.spec.ts — account, CSV mapping, import, rules, resolution, duplicates.
  • tests/e2e/matching.spec.ts — invoice, bill, payment, expense matches.
  • tests/e2e/reconciliation.spec.ts — start, clear, resume, complete, undo.
05

Reporting

Four read-only statements, operational analysis, the owner dashboard, and CSV/PDF exports that reuse the same report services.

statements · analysis · dashboard · export
  1. Period resolved
    Period reports default from to the fiscal-year start on or before to and to to today. As-of reports default to today. Every response echoes the effective period.
  2. Statements assemble
    ReportingEngine reads tenant-scoped ledger rows. P&L uses posted balances; the balance sheet adds a synthetic current-earnings equity row; trial balance splits net balances; cash flow is direct-method and excludes pure transfers. Each response exposes a balanced/reconciled check.
  3. Operational analysis
    Sales by customer/item and expenses by category use posted documents only. Sales are pre-tax with tax separate; expenses combine quick expenses and pre-tax bill lines. Rows expand to sources and deep-link to invoices, bills, and expenses.
  4. Dashboard aggregates
    GET /dashboard reuses AR/AP aging as of period end and derives cash, trend, recent activity, and attention counts. Trend buckets reconcile to P&L; the Overview is fully API-driven.
  5. Export
    GET /reports/:report/export?format=csv|pdf validates the slug, format, and date shape, then passes the same service result to RFC 4180 CSV or a ruled Daybook PDF. Files carry organization, title, period, generated timestamp, rows, totals, and summary.
Books & guarantees
Writes
None; reports are read-only.
Access
reports:read on every role.
Math
Decimal only; no float ledger math.
Scope
Seven core reports export; AR/AP aging stay live-only.

Agent prompt mode

A natural-language prompt becomes a queued job, a role-filtered tool call, and an append-only log line. Writes become proposals instead of silent book changes.

prompt · tool boundary · log
  1. Prompt submitted
    POST /agent/prompts validates the optional model alias against the server allowlist, appends a QUEUED AgentActionLog row with an AGT-#### reference, creates an AgentJob, and enqueues on agent-prompts.
  2. Worker re-enters tenant context
    The worker re-resolves the user's membership from the database, then runs inside TenantContextService.runAs. Scoped services are never called before that context exists. Failures retry with bounded exponential backoff plus jitter; rate limits are RETRYING, not hard failures.
  3. Tool call is chosen and validated
    The orchestrator offers only tools the role can use. Arguments are validated with class-validator (forbidNonWhitelisted); organization, tenant, SQL, query, code, and script keys are rejected. At most three tool calls and three rounds are allowed.
  4. Read result or write proposal
    Read tools (insight.query, report.generate) execute immediately. Write tools (invoice.create, automation.create) default to approve, so the orchestrator creates one PENDING AgentProposal with prepared tenant ids and a rationale.
  5. Answer, log, UI
    The final answer returns with its log reference. GET /agent/jobs/:id exposes status, attempts, model, response, error, logs, and proposals. The /agent Clerk workspace shows the transcript, status copy, and Activity detail. Logs are append-only and tenant-scoped.
Books & guarantees
Providers
Gemini or deterministic stub behind AiProviderService.
Models
flash, flash-lite, pro; unknown aliases 400.
Write cap
One write proposal per run.
Safety
No raw SQL, free-form code, or DB handle.
Evidence

  • tests/e2e/agent-approvals.spec.ts — write becomes pending.
  • backend/src/agent/agent-tools.spec.ts — validation and tenant scope.
07

Agent cron mode

A saved schedule registers one repeatable job keyed by automation id, runs a normal CRON job, and mirrors the last status back to the automation row.

create · scheduler · run · pause
  1. Automation created
    POST /agent/automations validates name, mode CRON, five-field cron, IANA timezone, prompt, and optional model. Unknown timezones and invalid cron are 400; duplicate names are 409. Creation is audited.
  2. One scheduler per automation
    AgentAutomationScheduler is the only repeatable-job writer. It uses upsertJobScheduler keyed by automation id, so edits replace rather than duplicate. Pause/delete remove the scheduler; startup re-registers enabled rows.
  3. Run enters the creator's context
    AgentAutomationRunner loads the row, verifies organization and enabled state, re-resolves the creator's membership, enters runAs, creates a CRON AgentJob, and enqueues it on agent-prompts.
  4. Status mirrored
    The prompt worker retries and logs normally, then mirrors RUNNING/RETRYING/COMPLETED/FAILED onto the automation with last run, status, job id, and log reference.
  5. Deterministic test path
    POST /agent/test/automations/:id/run exists only when AGENT_TEST_HOOKS=1; otherwise 404. A paused automation returns 409 even when the hook is on.
Books & guarantees
Modes
CRON or EVENT, immutable after create.
Schedule
Five fields plus IANA timezone; plain-language label.
Ownership
Runs as the creator, not the last viewer.
Queue
Redis/BullMQ in Compose; inline map in Jest.
Evidence
  • tests/e2e/agent-automations.spec.ts — create, edit, pause, run, Activity, delete.
  • backend/src/agent/agent-automation-scheduler.spec.ts — replace/remove/restore.
  • backend/src/agent/agent-automations.spec.ts — CRUD, RBAC, tenant, hook run.
08

Agent event mode

A committed business event routes only to enabled EVENT automations in the same organization that match its thresholds, then runs through the normal job path.

event bus · router · thresholds
  1. Business action commits
    The feature transaction commits and its audit rows are written first. The event is published only after commit; handler failures are logged and never fail the business action.
  2. Domain event published
    DomainEventsService carries { organizationId, type, entityType, entityId, occurredAt, data }. Types: BANK_TRANSACTION_IMPORTED, INVOICE_OVERDUE, BILL_OPENED, RECONCILIATION_DISCREPANCY.
  3. Same-org threshold match
    DomainEventRouter reads enabled EVENT rows for the event's organization and type and applies the pure matcher. Thresholds: daysOverdue (invoice, integer), minAmount (bill), and minDifference (reconciliation absolute). Bank events have no threshold.
  4. EVENT job runs
    runEvent re-resolves the creator, enters runAs, creates an EVENT AgentJob with an augmented event/record prompt, and enqueues on agent-prompts. Proposals and approvals follow the same policy as prompt mode.
  5. Deterministic firing
    POST /agent/test/events accepts { type, entityId?, data? }, is tenant-scoped, and is 404 unless AGENT_TEST_HOOKS=1. Paused rows never fire.
Books & guarantees
Ordering
Publish after commit and audit, never inside the tx.
Isolation
Router filters by event organization; no cross-tenant rows.
Defaults
Invoice 0 days, bill 0 amount, reconciliation 0 difference.
Reuse
Same queue, tools, logs, and approval path as prompts.
Evidence
  • tests/e2e/agent-event-automations.spec.ts — create threshold event, fire, Activity, delete.
  • backend/src/agent/agent-event-automations.spec.ts — bank fire, invoice days, bill/reconciliation thresholds.
  • backend/src/agent/domain-events-emission.spec.ts — bank, bill, discrepancy emission.

Approval flow

A sensitive action becomes a pending proposal. An Owner or Admin approves it exactly once, or rejects it with no books change. Auto-execute is the explicit per-organization variant.

proposal · approve once · reject · auto
  1. Sensitive action detected
    Tools declare readOnly, sensitive, and policy: auto | approve. invoice.create and automation.create are sensitive and default to approve. Read-only tools are fixed auto and not configurable.
  2. Pending proposal
    The orchestrator validates and prepares arguments, resolves names to exactly one active tenant customer/item, and creates one PENDING AgentProposal with requester, args, rationale, and log reference. No invoice or ledger row exists yet.
  3. Owner/Admin decides
    Approve re-checks the tool permission under the approver's role and tenant, row-locks PENDING, transitions to EXECUTING, then executes exactly once and stores EXECUTED/FAILED with decision and execution logs plus audit rows. Reject stores REJECTED and executes nothing.
  4. Repeat and concurrency
    Repeat or concurrent approvals return 409 after the row leaves PENDING. Staff, Accountant, and Viewer cannot approve or reject. Cross-tenant proposal ids are 404.
  5. Auto-execute variant
    An Owner/Admin can set the org override to AUTO. The next run reads the policy at execution time, executes through the internal approved path with normal tool/result logs, and creates no proposal. Switching back applies on the next run without a worker restart.
Books & guarantees
Approvers
Owner and Admin only (agent:approve).
Exactly once
Row lock + EXECUTING state before tool execution.
No change
Rejection and failed approval never touch the ledger.
Policies
Read at run time; changes audited agent.policy.update.
Evidence
  • tests/e2e/agent-approvals.spec.ts — propose, approve, open invoice; reject with no change.
  • tests/e2e/agent-policies.spec.ts — switch to auto, create without approval.
  • backend/src/agent/agent-approvals.spec.ts — concurrent 409 and exactly-once execution.

AI composer slash commands

A live listbox filters /model, /cron, and /event from the composer without bypassing permissions, tools, tenant scope, or approval policy.

/model · /cron · /event
  1. Menu opens
    The menu opens only when the prompt starts with / and contains no whitespace. It filters by prefix and exposes role=listbox with aria-activedescendant and aria-expanded.
  2. Keyboard or click
    ArrowUp/ArrowDown wrap, Enter selects, Escape dismisses, and clicking a row selects it. “No commands match” appears for a non-matching prefix.
  3. /model
    Opens the allowlisted model selector, updates session-only state, shows “Model: Flash Lite” in the composer meta, clears the composer, and sends the alias only on later prompts. Saved automations are untouched.
  4. /cron and /event
    Both require agent:manage. They switch to Automations, open the existing new-automation form preset to CRON or EVENT, and focus the name field. Nothing is created until the normal form submit. Non-managers see a permission message.
  5. Unknown command
    Shows “Unknown command. Try /model, /cron, or /event.” and clears the composer. It never sends the text to the model and never reaches a tool.
Books & guarantees
Scope
Composer UI only; no direct create or run.
Models
Server allowlist; unknown aliases rejected before a job.
Permission
/cron//event gate on agent:manage.
Motion
Composer pulse stops under reduced motion.
Evidence
  • tests/e2e/agent-slash-commands.spec.ts — 7 tests: filter, keys, click, model scope, trailing space, unknown, viewer, reduced motion.
  • backend/src/agent/agent.spec.ts — model list and alias propagation.
